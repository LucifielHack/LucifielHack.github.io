  1. Donut 已通过线上会议、脱敏报告、PPT 和 PoC 演示视频充分知晓 Critical 级资金安全攻击链。Through the online meetings, redacted report, slides, and PoC video, Donut was fully aware of the Critical-level fund-safety attack chain.
